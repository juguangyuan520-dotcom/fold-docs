# File Tray

[Open, select, and move files ←](file-operations.md) | [Guide home](../index.md) | [Search files and contents →](search.md) | [中文](../../files/file-tray.md)

Applies to Fold 0.4.6 and later.

## What It Solves

The file tray temporarily holds references to files. You can collect items from different folders, then drag them to a destination in one action. Adding an item does not move or copy the original file.

## Add to the Tray

Use either method:

- Drag selected files to the tray area at the top.
- Copy files or folders, open the tray, and press `⌘V`.
- Click Paste after expanding the tray at the top of the screen.

## View and Manage Items

- Click the counter drop at the top to switch between list, cards, and stack.
- List and card modes support rubber-band selection and dragging.
- Stack mode supports dragging the whole group.
- After a move is released, Undo may appear in the status bar.

## Floating Island

1. Enable Settings → General → Floating Island, or choose Fold → Screen-Top File Tray.
2. Drag files to the top of the screen to expand the tray.
3. Drag the handle in the upper-right corner to reposition it; double-click the handle to center it.
4. The position is saved per display.

## Limits

- The tray stores references, not independent copies.
- References can break if the original file is moved, renamed, or deleted.
- Whether an external drag moves or copies depends on the permissions and operations provided by the source app.
